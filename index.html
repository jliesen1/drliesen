About me

A career shaped by technology and education.

After working in healthcare information technology for 10 years, I came to Culver–Stockton College in February 1997 as Director of Information Technology, overseeing technology for both the administrative and academic sides of the institution.

In 2011, I also took on responsibility as Director of Facilities. I began teaching as an adjunct faculty member in 2016 and became a full-time faculty member in 2022 with the new Computer and Data Science program.

I'm a Certified Information Systems Security Professional (CISSP) and a certified Experiential Educator through the National Society for Experiential Education. In 2016, I received the college's President's Award for exemplary service.

Bringing years of technology experience into the classroom.
